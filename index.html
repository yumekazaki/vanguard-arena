ABOUT THIS BUILD

Online play, first steps.

This build models basic Premium turn rules, legal grade-based rides and calls, and trigger effects. It does not yet enforce every official Premium rule, deck restriction, or card-specific ability. Sign in to save decks to your account folder across devices.

Online rooms use Supabase Realtime so players can join from browsers without WebRTC support. Both players can use the match actions; spectators remain read-only.

Card art and imported card-text permissions still apply to any public deployment.
